8. ANIMATION STYLE
------------------

Animation Philosophy:
Minimal and premium.

Use:
- Fade up
- Soft reveal
- Staggered cards
- Gentle hover transitions

Avoid:
- Bounce effects
- Fast motion
- Heavy parallax
- Flashy transitions

Recommended Libraries:
- Framer Motion
- Lenis
- GSAP (optional)

--------------------------------------------------

9. IMAGE STYLE
--------------

Image Characteristics:
- Nature-based

- Cinematic lighting
- Calm atmosphere
- Soft green grading

Avoid:

- Overly bright colors
- Busy compositions

-----------------------

- Keep hero text compact
- CTA visible immediately
- Stack cards vertically
- Maintain large touch targets
- Avoid oversized typography
- Reduce spacing carefully
- Preserve hierarchy

Recommended Mobile Width:
390px

12. OVERALL DESIGN GOAL
-----------------------

The website should communicate:

- Authority
- Trust
- Clarity
- Calm confidence
- Premium coaching experience

The design should feel:
